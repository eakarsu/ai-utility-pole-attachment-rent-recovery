# Utility Pole Attachment Rent Recovery

Recover missing pole rent, unauthorized attachment fees, make-ready costs, and late payments from attachers.

**Primary buyer:** Electric utilities and pole owners. **Evidence:** pole inventories, attachment licenses, make-ready work, surveys, occupancy, attachment counts, rates, unauthorized attachments, invoices, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Pole inventory registry
- Attacher agreement library
- Attachment survey ingestion
- Authorized attachment matching
- Unauthorized attachment detection
- Rate formula calculation
- Space factor validation
- Carrying charge calculation
- Joint-use offset
- Make-ready cost recovery
- Back-rent calculation
- Attacher invoice generation
- Dispute workflow
- Cash reconciliation
- Territory attacher analytics
